Methodology

All entries are generated by a static Turtle-style trend-following system with fixed rules. The operator does not intervene in entries or exits. Where manual action was unavoidable, it appears above, in the incident log. The position size is $1,000 per position, up to three per market, on a paper account. Every closed trade above is auditable against three independent sources: the bot logs, the SQLite trade database, and the broker statement.
